Privacy Policy

This page explains what information placehold.us collects, who sees it, and why. The short version: there are no accounts, no forms and no ads, and we never sell anything about you. The homepage uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies. Images do not.

The homepage

The homepage uses Google Analytics to count visits. It sets Google Analytics cookies (named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID) in your browser. Google collects the pages you view, your browser and device type, the site that sent you, and your rough location (city or region). We use this to see how many people visit and where they come from. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy.

This privacy page does not use Google Analytics.

Images

Loading an image, on our site or anywhere else, never sets a cookie.

When we draw an image that is not already cached, we send Google Analytics one event with the image's size, format, scale and background type, and whether it has custom text (yes or no, never the text itself). The event carries an ID made by scrambling your IP address, browser type and the date with a secret key. It changes every day and can't be turned back into your IP address. Your IP address and browser type are not sent to Google. Images served from the cache send nothing.

The text you put in an image URL is part of the address, like any web address. It appears in our hosting logs and the image is cached for up to 30 days. Please don't put personal details in placeholder text.

Hosting

placehold.us runs on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser details with every request so it can deliver the page or image and protect the site from abuse. We use your IP address to limit each visitor to 100 new images per minute; that count lasts about a minute. Cloudflare keeps short-lived request logs that we can read when something breaks. Cloudflare handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. Images keep working either way.

Children

placehold.us is a tool for people who build websites. It is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information about them.

Changes

If what we collect changes, we will update this page and the date below.
